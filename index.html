Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Magic Editor ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Photos and Media Storage

Our app requires access to your device's Photo Gallery, Camera, and Storage solely to enable you to select, edit, and save photos onto your device.

  • Local Processing: All image editing and photo manipulation are processed entirely on your device.
  • We do not upload, collect, or store your personal photos or edited media on any external servers.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

To support app functionality, deliver updates, and provide advertisements, we use the following third-party services which may collect certain device identifiers or usage data:

  • Google Play Services: Core platform services.
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device advertising IDs and location data to show personalized or non-personalized ads. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Analytics & Remote Config: Used to analyze app performance, user engagement, and deliver feature configurations. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to deliver push notifications and updates to your device.

3. Permissions Used

  • Read & Write Storage / Media Access: To load images for editing and save finished photos to your gallery.
  • Camera: To take photos directly inside the app for editing.
  • Internet Access: To load ads, receive configuration updates, and fetch analytics.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any modifications are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.
